STMC journey

Five years of progress.

A focused timeline of documented contributions, from clinical advocacy to advanced gait analysis and service development.

2022

Foundation & advocacy

  • Joined as a physiotherapist and progressively focused on neurological rehabilitation.
  • Proposed increasing complex neurological appointment slots from 40 to 60 minutes.
  • Completed vestibular orientation and presented advanced rehabilitation equipment applications.
2023

Clinical specialization & governance

  • Managed a substantial share of outpatient neurological cases.
  • Presented PAR programme classifications and a coordinated rehabilitation model.
  • Supported infection-control documentation audits and fire and safety work.
2024

Launching services

  • Supported outpatient hydrotherapy and robotics launches, policies, clinical workflows and referrals.
  • Reported full hydrotherapy utilization; initiated neurological multidisciplinary discussions.
  • Led robotics workflow design and therapist training; documented a spinal cord injury recovery case for service promotion.
2025

Optimization & measurable impact

  • Dedicated 60-minute neurological appointment slot implemented in February.
  • Managed 800+ neurological cases and 80 vestibular cases; allocated 85%+ of appointments to neuro/vestibular care.
  • Continued robotics competency training and submitted an outpatient quality-improvement proposal.
2026

Advanced gait analysis & quality capability

  • Completed Qualisys training and performed gait assessments with clinical interpretation and reporting.
  • Supported gait lab workflows, physician referrals, business and revenue planning, and service promotion.
  • Delivered a computerized dynamic posturography workshop, continued robotics training and reported 100% patient satisfaction.
